About This Calculator

A free, no-signup tool for working out body mass index.

What it does

This site provides a single, focused tool: a body mass index calculator that accepts metric or imperial input. Alongside your BMI, it shows the World Health Organization weight category and the healthy weight range for your height.

How it calculates

In metric units, BMI is weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared. In imperial units, BMI is 703 multiplied by weight in pounds, divided by height in inches squared. The weight categories follow the standard WHO adult thresholds: under 18.5 underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 healthy weight, 25.0 to 29.9 overweight, and 30.0 and above obese.

All calculations run in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to a server.

Who it is for

This calculator is intended for adults aged 20 and over. It is not suitable for children, teenagers, pregnant women, or people whose body composition differs markedly from the average — for example, competitive athletes. Those groups should use age- and situation-appropriate assessment instead.

What it is not

BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. It does not measure body fat, it does not describe where fat is stored, and it cannot account for muscle mass, bone density, or individual medical history. If you have concerns about your weight or your health, please speak to a qualified healthcare professional.

This calculator is provided for general informational purposes only and is not medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your health.
