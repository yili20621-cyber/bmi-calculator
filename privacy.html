Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Information you enter

The height and weight you type into the calculator are processed entirely in your browser. They are never transmitted to our servers, never stored, and never shared. Closing the page erases them.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we may collect standard technical information when you visit: your approximate location derived from IP address, browser and device type, referring page, and the pages you view. This information is aggregated and used to understand how the site is used and to keep it working properly.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device. This site may use cookies for two purposes: measuring traffic (analytics) and, where applicable, serving advertising. You can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Doing so does not affect the calculator, which works without cookies.

Analytics

We use a third-party analytics service to understand aggregate visitor behaviour — for example, how many people visit, which pages they read, and which countries they come from. Analytics data is not used to identify you personally.

Advertising

This site may display advertising supplied by third-party networks. Advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising through the settings offered by those networks or through your browser's privacy controls.

Third-party links

The site may link to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Your choices

You can browse this site without providing any personal information. If you would like to know what data has been collected about your visit, or wish to raise a privacy concern, please use the contact page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the "last updated" date above will be revised accordingly.

This calculator is provided for general informational purposes only and is not medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your health.
